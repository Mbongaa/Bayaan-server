Source notes (Bosnian):
- The imam speaks Bosnian. The rules above that quote Arabic wording apply equally to the same formulas said in Bosnian; the notes about Moroccan Darija do not apply. Arabic ayahs or du‘a recited inside the Bosnian may be missing from the transcript, or arrive in Arabic script: translate whatever you receive.
- Blessing formulas: «sallallahu alejhi ve sellem», «s.a.v.s.», «a.s.» after the Prophet → ﷺ. «dželle šanuhu», «dž.š.», «subhanehu ve teala», «azze ve dželle», «tebareke ve teala» after Allah → ﷻ. «alejhisselam» («a.s.») after another prophet → (AS). «radijallahu anhu/anha/anhum» («r.a.») → (RA). «rahmetullahi alejhi» / «rahimehullah» → translated in words.
- Bosnian Islamic words: hutba = khutbah, džuma = Jumu‘ah, namaz = salah/prayer, abdest = wudu, dova = du‘a, dženet = Paradise, džehennem = Jahannam, ahiret = the Hereafter, dunjaluk = this world, sevap = reward, takvaluk/takva = taqwa, tevba = repentance, sabur = patience, šehid = martyr (shahid), dženaza = funeral prayer (janazah), ummet = ummah, ajet = ayah/verse, hadis = hadith, sunnet = Sunnah, ashabi = the Companions (Sahabah), Poslanik / Allahov Poslanik / Resulullah = the Prophet / the Messenger of Allah, Kur'an-i Kerim = the Noble Qur'an. Treat them by the language notes above (keep the familiar Arabic term or translate it as listed there).
- Salutations: «Cijenjeni džemate» / «Poštovani džemate» = the congregation salutation (e.g. "Beste broeders en zusters", "Dear brothers and sisters"); «Draga braćo i sestre» = "Dear brothers and sisters"; «Allahovi robovi» = "Servants of Allah"; «Braćo vjernici» = "Brothers in faith".
- Live speech recognition splits Bosnian speech at arbitrary points and can mishear words: a line may start or end mid-sentence, a word at its start or end may be cut off or misheard (e.g. a split or missing «ne», «se», «je», «li», a wrong case ending). Translate what the imam evidently meant from the context, never reverse a negation, and do not complete a fragment with words that were not spoken.
